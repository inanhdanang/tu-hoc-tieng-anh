Mô tả đồ ăn – Tính từ & Cấu trúc câu

hot · cold · fresh · wilted · delicious · sour · sweet · spicy · và nhiều hơn
📌 Hôm nay học gì?
🍽️ Tính từ mô tả đồ ăn: hot, cold, fresh, wilted, delicious, sour, sweet, spicy, salty, bland, crispy, soft, oily, healthy...
📐 Cấu trúc: S + is/are + (very/too) + Adj  |  S + looks/tastes/smells + Adj
🗣️ Hỏi ý kiến: How is the food? / What does it taste like?

✅ Mô tả với IS/ARE

S + is/are + (very/too) + Adj
→ This coffee is very hot.
→ The soup is too hot.
→ The soup is cold.
→ This fish is fresh.

👁️ Mô tả với LOOK/TASTE/SMELL

S + looks/tastes/smells + Adj
→ This food looks delicious.
→ This bunch looks too wilted.
→ The soup tastes sour.
→ The bread smells fresh.

❓ Hỏi Yes/No về đồ ăn

Is/Are + S + Adj?
→ Is the food cold?
→ Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
→ Is this dish spicy?
→ Are the vegetables fresh?

❓ Hỏi ý kiến / mô tả

How is the + N? / What does it taste like?
→ How is the soup?
→ It's delicious!
→ What does it taste like?
→ It tastes a bit sour.
💡 Phân biệt very / too:
• very = rất (tích cực) → This coffee is very hot. (nóng, dễ chịu)
• too = quá (tiêu cực, vấn đề) → The soup is too hot. (nóng quá, không ăn được)
• a bit / a little = hơi → This is a bit salty.
⚠️ Lỗi hay gặp:
❌ This food very delicious.  →  ✅ This food is very delicious. (phải có "is")
❌ The soup taste sour.  →  ✅ The soup tastes sour. (he/she/it → thêm -s)
❌ Too delicious!  →  ✅ Very delicious! (too = tiêu cực, dùng very cho khen ngợi)
