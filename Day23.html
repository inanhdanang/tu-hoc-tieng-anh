Ôn luyện & Mở rộng Động từ Bất quy tắc

6 động từ mới + ôn 6 động từ đã học
📌 Hôm nay học gì?
🆕 6 động từ MỚI: find/found · write/wrote · think/thought · know/knew · sleep/slept · send/sent
🔁 Ôn 6 động từ cũ: eat/ate · drink/drank · go/went · buy/bought · meet/met · make/made

✅ Khẳng định – dùng V2

S + V2 + ...
→ I found my keys. (not "finded")
→ He wrote a book 3 years ago.
→ She thought it was funny.

❌ Phủ định – didn't + V1

S + didn't + V1 (nguyên mẫu)
→ I didn't find my keys.
→ He didn't write anything.
→ Luôn dùng V1 sau "didn't"!

❓ Câu hỏi – Did + V1

Did + S + V1?
→ Did you find your wallet?
→ Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
→ Did she write a report?

❓ Câu hỏi WH-

WH- + did + S + V1?
→ What did you find?
→ Where did he go?
→ When did she send it?
⚠️ Lỗi hay gặp – KHÔNG thêm "-ed":
❌ I finded my bag.  →  ✅ I found my bag.
❌ She writed a letter.  →  ✅ She wrote a letter.
❌ He thinked about it.  →  ✅ He thought about it.
💡 Mẹo nhớ theo nhóm:
• find→found / bind→bound / wind→wound (đổi -ind → -ound)
• write→wrote / drive→drove / ride→rode (đổi -ite → -ote)
• think→thought / bring→brought / buy→bought (đuôi -ought)
