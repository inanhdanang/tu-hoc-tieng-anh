DIDN'T & DID – Phủ định & Câu hỏi Quá Khứ

Câu phủ định · Yes/No question · WH-question
📌 Hôm nay học gì? Ba cấu trúc quan trọng trong thì quá khứ đơn: didn't (phủ định), Did...? (câu hỏi có/không), WH + did...? (câu hỏi lấy thông tin)[cite: 40].

❌ Câu phủ định

S + didn't + V (nguyên thể)
I didn't go to work yesterday.
She didn't watch TV last night.
He didn't call me.
He didn't read books last night.
✅ didn't = did not[cite: 40]
✅ Động từ SAU didn't → nguyên thể (không chia)[cite: 40]

❓ Câu hỏi Yes/No

Did + S + V (nguyên thể)?
Did you go to work yesterday?
Did she watch TV last night?
Did you read books yesterday?
Did they cook dinner last night[cite: 39]?
✅ Trả lời: Yes, I did / No, I didn't[cite: 40].
✅ Động từ SAU did → nguyên thể[cite: 40]

🔍 Câu hỏi WH

WH + did + S + V (nguyên thể)?
Where did you go yesterday?
What did she watch last night?
What did you read yesterday?
When did they watch this movie?
✅ WH: Where · What · When · Why · How · Who[cite: 40]
✅ Động từ vẫn là nguyên thể[cite: 40]

💬 Hỏi & Đáp mẫu

Q → A đầy đủ
Did you go to work yesterday?
→ No, I didn't. I stayed at home.

Did he call you yesterday[cite: 39]?
→ He didn't call me. He was busy.

What did she watch last night?
→ She didn't watch anything. She studied English.
⚠️ Lỗi thường gặp:
❌ "I didn't went to work" → ✅ "I didn't go to work" (nguyên thể!)[cite: 40]
❌ "She didn't watched TV" → ✅ "She didn't watch TV"[cite: 40]
❌ "Did you went?" → ✅ "Did you go?" (nguyên thể!)[cite: 40]
❌ "Where did you went?" → ✅ "Where did you go?"[cite: 40]
❌ "He not call me." → ✅ "He didn't call me."[cite: 40]
💡 Mẹo nhớ nhanh:
Hễ có did / didn't trong câu → động từ theo sau luôn là nguyên thể (không "s", không "ed", không "ing")[cite: 40].
didn't = did + not (viết tắt) · Dùng cho TẤT CẢ chủ ngữ (I, you, he, she, they, we)[cite: 40]
📝 Các trạng từ thời gian hay dùng:
yesterday – hôm qua · last night – tối qua · last week – tuần trước[cite: 40]
this morning – sáng nay · last year – năm ngoái · ago – trước đây[cite: 40]
