Trang phục trong Tiếng Anh – Cấu trúc & Từ vựng

wear · put on · take off · cách mô tả quần áo đang mặc
📌 Hôm nay học gì? Từ vựng quần áo: jeans, dress, pants, shorts, raincoat + nhiều hơn. Cách nói đang mặc (wear/wearing), thường mặc, mua quần áo, và mô tả trang phục.

👔 Đang mặc (hiện tại)

S + am/is/are + wearing + clothes
I am wearing jeans today.
He is wearing blue shorts.
She is wearing a white dress.
They are wearing uniforms.

👕 Thường mặc (hiện tại)

S + wear/wears + clothes
I usually wear black pants to work.
She wears a dress to school.
I don't usually wear blue jeans to work.
He never wears shorts in winter.

🛍️ Mua / Có quần áo

S + bought / have + clothes
My mother bought a black dress.
I don't have a raincoat.
She has many dresses.
I need to buy new shoes.

📐 Mô tả trang phục

S + is/are + too + Adj + for me
This dress is too small for me.
These pants are too tight.
This shirt is too big.
These shoes are too expensive.
💡 Lưu ý quan trọng:
• Quần áo số nhiều: pants, jeans, shorts, trousers → dùng are / these
→ These pants are too tight. ✅    This pants is... ❌
• Dress, shirt, coat: số ít → dùng is / this
→ This dress is too small. ✅
• wear (V1, thói quen) vs wearing (đang mặc ngay lúc này)
⚠️ Lỗi thường gặp:
❌ "I wear jeans now." → ✅ "I am wearing jeans now."
❌ "This pants is too tight." → ✅ "These pants are too tight."
❌ "I wearing a dress." → ✅ "I am wearing a dress."
❌ "She is wear a coat." → ✅ "She is wearing a coat."
